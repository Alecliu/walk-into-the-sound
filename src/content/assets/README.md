# Authored content assets

Place user-approved logos, illustrations, and custom content icons here. Product-owned chrome icons and protected runtime assets must remain unchanged.

## Record-store design, 2026-09-29

Selected concept: third displayed option, warm yellow / forest-green record store.
The four raster assets were generated with built-in ImageGen and are decorative, not actual album covers:
- `record-sky.png`: cobalt sky and intersecting coral stucco walls, palm frond, sunny analog photograph.
- `record-coral.png`: curved coral wall, dark palm shadows, cobalt sky sliver.
- `record-green.png`: green stucco wall, coral window recess, palm shadows.
- `good-music.png`: transparent green brush lettering, “Good Music Lives Here.”

The homepage labels the three photos as illustrative. The artwork is not used as album art in the chart table.
Icons are unmodified Phosphor regular SVGs from https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core, with MIT license in `icons/LICENSE`.
The headline uses a self-hosted Noto Sans TC 900 subset from Google Fonts for “今天，先聽這幾首。”, with `FONT-OFL.txt`.
